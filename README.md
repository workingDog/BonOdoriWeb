# BonOdori Web


**BonOdori** is a clean music player designed specifically for Bon Odori dance practice and instruction.

The app allows Bon Odori dance instructors to create playlists of songs and play them during dance classes. Large, easy-to-use controls make it quick to start, stop, pause, rewind, or move through a song while the instructor is teaching.
The app includes a Demo Playlist with computer generated music examples, allowing users to try the app’s music playback and playlist functionality.
The demo music tracks were created specifically for the app using computer-generated audio. They are original compositions and recordings, not copied or sampled from an existing commercial or copyrighted recording.

Additional songs can be imported into the app and added to playlists for use during practice sessions and classes.

**BonOdori** is designed to keep the instructor’s attention on the dancers rather than on operating the music player.

**BonOdori** is free to use and does not provide or sell music. It is a tool for playing music selected by the user for Bon Odori dance practice and instruction. The app requires no account, registration, subscription, payment information, internet connection, or advertising.
